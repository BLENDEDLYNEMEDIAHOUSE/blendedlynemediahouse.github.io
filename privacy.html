Privacy policy — BMH n8n Local

Effective 2026-11-20.

Who uses this app

BMH n8n Local is a private tool used only by its owner. It is not available to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own Google Drive files and Google Sheets, and sends email from the owner's own Gmail account to the owner. It only accesses the account of the person who signed in.

How the data is used

Data is used only to run the owner's scheduled automations, such as a nightly check of the owner's own files and a summary email to the owner. Data is processed on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data is collected from anyone other than the owner.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

The app keeps no separate copy of Google data beyond what the owner's own automations store on the owner's computer. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
